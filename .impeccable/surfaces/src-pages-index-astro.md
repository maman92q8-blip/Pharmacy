Scope: site-wide elevation of the existing RXperiences world (extension, not replacement). Modes: homepage Persuade-leaning Read (a publication front page); article, blog, category, library, policies, resources: Read. Build path: code-led (no image generation).

Audience/job: Gulf pharmacy students on phones looking for a first-hand experience matching their plan; pharmacists looking up MoH decisions and research. Success: find a relevant story fast, read it comfortably, continue to the next or book a consultation.

Constraints: keep URLs, content, search/filter scripts, SEO meta, GitHub-only authoring. No invented claims or medical content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm Kuwaiti pharmacy magazine, not a card blog. Stories are set like a publication (lead story, rails, chapters) and the brand's sticker world (navy outline, yellow/pink, Hawan and Habba) frames them without crowding the reading column. Refuses the incumbent default of the same bordered, offset-shadow card grid for every section.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground #fcfaf7, ink navy #1e2a4a, Rx pink #c43c6e, sunny #f7c948, blush #fbe3ec, lilac #8e9bc0. Rubik is the brand/UI voice; Noto Naskh Arabic is the story voice (every article title and the reading column; English words fall back to Rubik). Markazi Text was tried first and dropped after review: its Arabic was too close to Rubik to read as a second voice. Category colour bands like medicine-box coding: UK/Ireland lilac, USA/Canada pink, Arab countries sunny, articles navy. Navy outline + small offset shadow reserved for the mascots and primary buttons only.

STORY: A student sees immediately this is the place for real pharmacy study and career experiences, jumps to their region or searches, reads a story in comfort, and moves on to the next story or a consultation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: navy masthead field; right column big Rubik headline, one-line lead, a search field and three region links ("where do you want to study?"); left column Hawan and Habba large on a sunny blob. Below the fold edge, the lead story begins. Article: category band label, Naskh title at display size, date and reading time, then a matted cover and the reading column at 64ch.

FORM: Editorial front page and reading layout inside the incumbent sticker brand; first choice on the ordered list (extension of the established world, no roll). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
